## 🚀 About Me

Software Engineer with nearly 5 years of experience designing, developing and modernizing enterprise applications.

My expertise is centered around the .NET ecosystem, applying architectural patterns such as Domain-Driven Design (DDD), Clean Architecture, Onion Architecture, Microservices and Event-Driven Architecture (EDA) to create maintainable and scalable systems.

I have extensive experience working with high-throughput messaging platforms such as RabbitMQ and Kafka, building distributed systems capable of supporting mission-critical business operations.

Recently, I have been focused on integrating Generative AI into real-world products, developing AI Agents and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) solutions using Azure and AWS ecosystems.
